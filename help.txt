PDB Analyzer (v1.3)

It is a Streamlit-based web application for analyzing PDB files, exploring interface analysis, and visualizing 3D protein structures.

Step 1: Define the target and binder chains. 
Step 2: Clarify the residue numbering offset between the initial target PDB and the final output. 
Step 3: Upload the PDB files.

After processing, the app provides seven tabs: 

1. Summary:
- Displays all analysed results in an interactive table.  

2. Visualisations: 
- Interactive 3D structure viewer using py3Dmol. 
- Customizable chain colouring. 
- Counts and plots the number of 3 Å, 4 Å contacts, hydrophobic contacts, salt bridges, and H-bonds between binder and target for each design. 

3. Details: 
- For each design, you can inspect:
    • Target and binder sequences
    • Residue–residue contact pairs (3 Å and 4 Å) and distance 
    • Salt bridges
    • Hydrophobic contacts
    • H-bonds
    • Pi-Pi contacts
    • Cation-Pi contacts

4. Binder Analysis:
- For each designed binder, you can inspect:
    • Binder length & sequence
    • Hydrophobicity analysis

5. Binder Analysis Overall:
- Displays hydrophobicity, acidic, and basic percentages of all designed binders.  

6. Overall:
- Displays the frequency of target residues at the interface (3 Å, 4 Å, hydrophobic contacts, and salt bridges).

7. Filters (Customization):
- You can choose your best designs based on your customized filters.
